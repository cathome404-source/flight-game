# Flight Game — Prose Spec (best-flight-game-20261126-0057)

Build a complete original 3D flight game in a desktop browser from static files, no network request. Target: the make.fun judge's first criterion (a complete flight game in screenshots) plus fun/depth, feel/polish, look/originality.

## Concept

"Garuda Skies" — a low-poly 3D flight game set over a tropical island archipelago at golden hour. The player pilots a small single-propeller plane. One consistent warm low-poly style for terrain, sky, sea and aircraft.

## What the player sees (judge criteria first)

- A chase view (default) and a cockpit view (toggle with C). The cockpit view shows an instrument panel with lit gauges.
- Recognisable terrain: a sea, several islands with beaches, palm trees, low hills/mountains, and a coastal runway with lights.
- The aircraft in frame, visibly built (fuselage, wings, tail, spinning propeller, navigation lights).
- A runway or a target ring ahead in the main views.
- A HUD showing altitude, airspeed, heading (compass), throttle, fuel, vertical speed, mission objective, timer and score.

## Controls (keyboard + mouse)

- Arrow keys or WASD: pitch and roll (bank to turn).
- W/S or Shift/Ctrl: throttle up/down.
- C: toggle cockpit/chase camera.
- Mouse: drag to look around (camera orbit in chase view).
- R: restart the current mission. P or Esc: pause. M: mute.

## Flight model (feel + depth)

- Lift depends on airspeed: below a stall speed the nose drops and a stall warning shows; above it the plane flies cleanly.
- Turn radius depends on speed and bank: slow + steep bank turns tight, fast + shallow turns wide. Turning well is a skill.
- Throttle controls acceleration; drag slows the plane when throttle is low.
- Fuel depletes with throttle use. Fuel rings and landing refuel. Running out of fuel means gliding to a forced landing.
- Damage from hard landings and terrain hits. A gentle touchdown is safe; a hard one damages the plane; 100% damage is a crash. Landings and crashes must read clearly (bounce, dust/smoke, screen shake, sound).

## Missions (5, different goals)

1. Takeoff — get airborne from the runway and climb past 100 m.
2. Ring Run — fly through 8 target rings in order.
3. Island Tour — visit 3 checkpoints over different islands.
4. Precision Landing — land inside the marked touchdown zone on the runway.
5. Storm Run — fly through 6 rings against the clock as wind picks up.

Each mission shows its objective on the HUD, a marker/arrow pointing to the next target, and a completion banner with time and score. Completing a mission unlocks the next. A free-flight mode is available from the menu.

## Audio (procedural, WebAudio, no files)

- Engine hum whose pitch follows throttle, wind noise that follows airspeed, a chime when passing a ring, a thud on touchdown, a crash sound, and a short success jingle on mission complete. Mute toggle.

## Tech

- Three.js r149 vendored as game/vendor/three.min.js (a minified build, so it is listed as vendored and never counted as source). All other code is original, written fresh in recorded sessions from this spec.
- ES modules under game/src/, one clear job per file. No network request at runtime: every script, image, sound and font is a file under game/ or a data: URI.
- The game runs from static files: game/index.html opens in desktop Chrome and plays.
